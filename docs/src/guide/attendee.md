# Attendee guide

You do not need an account or an app. Type the event's **room code** into the six boxes on the home page (it is shown as
two triplets, like `ABC-123`, and never changes during the event), or scan the organiser's QR code with your camera.
Both lead to the same page, so remote classes can join with the code alone.

![The home page with the join-by-code box](/screens/landing.png){width=280}

## Joining

To add songs, type **your name** and tap **Join this event**. The name is required, so the organiser can see who added which song;
other attendees do not see it in the queue. Your device gets a private pass for this event only, kept in your browser.

![The event page before joining](/screens/attendee-join.png){width=280}

## The event page

![The event page for an attendee](/screens/attendee-event.png){width=280}

- **Status pill (top right of the deck):** _Live_ means updates arrive instantly. _Reconnecting_ means the app is
  checking every 10 seconds. _Offline_ means you are seeing the last update; check your signal. It also says _Paused_,
  _On a break_, _Not started_ or _Ended_ when that is what the event is doing.
- **Now playing** (the wide card) shows the current song, its genre and, when it matters, the style run (for example
  "1/2"). **Next up** (the narrow card) shows the next song in the queue, with `~N min` only when the queue can predict it.
  Neither card can be changed by a guest; only the organiser moves the music on.
- **My next song:** how many songs are ahead of yours, and a ticking `~MM:SS`. It says _Estimated_ when a song
  length is unknown. The timer is based on the server's clock, not your phone's.
- **Whole queue:** a collapsed list of every title in play order. Your songs are tagged _Yours_. Songs that would run past
  the planned end are greyed and labelled _After end time_; they are not gone, and may play if the end time is extended.

### When there is no countdown

| You see                                | It means                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session paused                         | The organiser paused the event; your place is kept                        |
| Waiting for more eligible songs        | The genre limit was reached; a song of another genre is needed            |
| Position not yet predictable           | Your song is held until earlier songs play                                |
| Beyond planned end — admin must extend | The event would finish before your song                                   |
| Possible duplicate — needs review      | It may be the same recording as one already queued; the organiser decides |

## Adding music

There is one place to add music, for a playlist or a single song.

1. Search Spotify in the box and tap a song, or paste a link from Spotify. If your account is on the pilot list, you can also log in and choose one of your own playlists. Long-press the box to paste, or tap **Paste**, which reads your clipboard only when you tap it. Title, artist and length come from the link.
2. Pick the link's **genre**. Qroove then reminds you to remove songs of other genres, for example _You selected SALSA. Remove all BACHATA, CHA-CHA, OTHER songs._
3. Sort the list: drag to reorder, swipe left to remove, swipe right to send to the top. Songs beyond your allowance are greyed out.
4. Tap **Add**.

If something goes wrong the message says what to do next, for example _Already queued as "…". Pick a different
song or version._ A song can only be in an event once, even from a different platform.

## Adding a whole playlist

Tap **♫ +** and paste a public Spotify, YouTube Music, YouTube or Apple Music playlist link. Its songs appear as a
list you can sort before anything is added:

- Only as many songs as you have room for can be added (the first ones); the rest are greyed out, below a dashed line.
- **Drag** a song by its grip (⠿) up or down to put it where you want it.
- **Swipe left** to remove it: a red bin appears as you go. Let go early and it springs back.
- **Swipe right** on any song except the first to send it straight to the top: a green arrow appears and the song
  slides up.
- Buttons for each of these appear when you tab to a song, for keyboard and screen-reader use.

The card shows how many of your accepted songs you have used (for example _2 of 5 accepted songs_). Pick one **genre**
for the songs (Salsa, Bachata, Cha-cha or Other, whichever the event plays); the line under the chips says what the
choice means, for example which other genres' songs to remove. Then tap **Add N songs to queue**. Songs already in the event are skipped and do not use up
your limit. Playlist pages are public web pages, so this is best effort and long playlists show only their first part.

## My songs and the five-song limit

The **My songs** card lists your songs in the order they will play, the one playing now highlighted, each with its
genre and status, and `Est. in ~N min` when the queue can predict it. Finished songs are under **Played songs (n)**, a
button that opens and closes the list.

You can have five accepted songs per event. Songs that have played, or were skipped after starting, still count.
Removing a song that has **not started** with **Withdraw** frees its slot straight away. A song that is playing
or has played cannot be withdrawn.

## Light and dark

The button at the top right switches between dark (default, for dim rooms) and light.
